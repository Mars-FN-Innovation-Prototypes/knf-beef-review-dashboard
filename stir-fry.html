Methodology and data qualityScope, dates, retailer matching, disclosure, deduplication, and limits+
Official scope

Thirteen products were taken from the Kevin's Stir-Fry Entrées collection and verified to official product handles and catalog identifiers.

Cohorts

Six 2.5-serving grocery kits launched nationally on Feb 27, 2025. Seven four-serving items are treated as Costco-only.

Written evidence

331 deduplicated dated comments: 81 owned-site, 56 incremental Target, 29 incremental Kroger, 162 incremental Thrive Market, and 3 native Meijer records.

Comparable trend

Monthly and launch-window trends use the complete 81-review first-party history. The complete visible Target and Kroger histories add cross-channel issue discovery without changing that comparison base.

Expanded sources

Three exact-UPC Thrive pages returned 164 public written rows; two within-source duplicates were removed, leaving 162 incremental records. One exact Meijer listing contributed three retailer-native comments after syndicated owned-site rows were excluded.

Rating context

Channel totals are shown separately and may overlap through syndication. Thrive rating totals include rating-only responses; its displayed 1–2 star mix uses written reviews. Amazon produced no confirmed exact scoped page.

Disclosure

Future-purchase and Thrive Cash disclosures are preserved on individual rows and can be excluded. Explicit sponsorship remains separate; no undisclosed sponsorship is inferred.

Retailer matching

Products were matched with official names, pack architecture, barcodes where available, retailer identifiers, ingredients, and product imagery. Adjacent Amazon sauces and Heat & Eat products were excluded.

Value-for-money coding

Separate complaint rules classify insufficient protein, insufficient vegetables, serving-size inadequacy, and explicit negative price/value tension. Multi-tagging is allowed and all coded reviews remain inspectable.

Topic coding

Broader keyword rules also tag taste, portion/value, protein quantity, vegetables, texture, convenience, dietary fit, and packaging.

Interpretation

Review signals identify where to investigate. They do not prove formulation, supplier, production, or channel causality.
